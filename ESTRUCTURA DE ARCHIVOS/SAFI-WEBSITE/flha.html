Field Level Hazard Assessment (FLHA)

Complete this checklist before starting work. Mark each item Yes / No / N/A and sign.

1. Project & worker information

Few basics only – the rest is checklist.

2. Hazards – See it / Evaluate it / Control it

For each item select: Yes = conditions are acceptable / controls in place, No = problem, fix before work, N/A = not applicable to today’s work.

A. Site conditions & housekeeping
Housekeeping acceptable? (no tripping hazards, debris controlled)
Walking surfaces in good condition? (no ice, holes, unsecured covers)
Lighting adequate in work area?
Weather conditions considered and controls in place? (cold, heat, wind, storm)
B. Access, ladders, scaffolds & working at heights
Access / stairs / work platforms safe and in good condition?
Ladders inspected and in good condition?
Scaffolds / platforms inspected, tagged and complete (guardrails, planks, access)?
Working at heights with proper fall protection (harness, anchor, guardrails)?
Open edges, floor openings, shafts and roof edges properly guarded?
C. Tools, equipment & machinery
Power cords and tools undamaged and in good condition?
Guards and safety devices in place on tools / equipment?
Defective tools / equipment tagged out and removed from service?
Mobile equipment (zoom boom, skid steer, forklifts, etc.) in good condition and inspected?
Hoisting / crane / overhead loads controlled and exclusion zones in place?
D. Electrical, underground & confined space
Overhead power lines and electrical panels identified and clearances maintained?
Lockout / tagout in place for energized systems where required?
Underground services (gas, power, water, telecom) located before digging?
Excavations / trenches properly sloped, shored, barricaded and access provided?
Confined spaces identified and proper entry permit / testing in place?
E. Chemicals, dust, noise & air quality
Chemicals / products labelled and used as per WHMIS / SDS?
Asbestos, silica or other hazardous materials identified and controls in place?
Dust, fumes, vapours controlled (ventilation, wet methods, respirators)?
Noise levels acceptable or hearing protection used if required?
F. Manual handling & ergonomics
Lifting / carrying tasks planned to avoid overexertion (team lifts, equipment)?
Awkward postures / repetitive tasks minimized where possible?
G. Traffic, public & other trades
Vehicle / equipment traffic controlled (spotters, barricades, routes)?
Public / building occupants protected from work hazards?
Other trades / contractors coordinated to avoid conflicts (overhead work, same area)?
H. PPE & personal factors
Required PPE available and being used (hard hat, hi-vis, boots, gloves, eye/ear, respirator)?
Workers fit for duty (no impairment from drugs, alcohol, fatigue, etc.)?
Young / new / inexperienced workers given extra supervision?
Violence, harassment or working alone risks considered and controls in place?
I. Environment & emergency preparedness
Fuel, oils, paints and other materials stored to prevent spills / environmental release?
Wildlife or pests considered (remote sites) and controls in place if required?
Emergency exits, routes and muster point known and kept clear?
Fire extinguishers, first aid kit and eye wash available and accessible?
Communication available in case of emergency (radio, phone, contact list)?
Permit(s) in place if required (hot work, confined space, ground disturbance, etc.)?

3. Extra hazards / notes (optional)

Use only if there is something special today (extra controls, permits, client instructions, etc.).

4. Worker sign-off

I confirm I have reviewed the hazards, controls and will stop work and contact my supervisor if conditions change.

Tip: rotate your phone for more space if needed.
